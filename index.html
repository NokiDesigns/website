Hello—I’m Derrick McGill, a recent graduate of the Visual College of Art and Design (VCAD) and the creative engine behind Noki Designs. As a hands on video game developer, I tackle every stage of production—from high quality 3D modeling, scene/environment creation, level design, and asset integration to gameplay scripting, lighting, and performance optimization. Working in engines like Unreal and Unity (and even custom Three.js builds), I sculpt, texture, and animate assets, implement core mechanics, and polish experiences for maximum immersion. Everything except conceptual art falls into my scope of ability, and I’m passionate about transforming ideas into seamless, playable worlds.
